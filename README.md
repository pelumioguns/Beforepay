# Beforepay
Fraud/Scam checking tool


### Check before you send.

Every day, people lose money to fake sellers, fake jobs, fake investments, fake websites, hacked accounts, and convincing messages.

**BeforePay helps you stop and check before your money leaves your hands.**

Paste a suspicious message, phone number, website, or payment request into BeforePay. We'll look for common scam warning signs and explain, in simple language, what you should be worried about.

### Don't guess. Check.

Before you:

* Send money
* Buy from an unfamiliar seller
* Pay for a job or opportunity
* Invest your money
* Click a suspicious link
* Trust a stranger online

**Check it with BeforePay first.**

Because once the money is gone, it's usually too late.
